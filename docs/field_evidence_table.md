# Research Evidence Table — Violence Exposure, Depression, and Cardiovascular/Allostatic-Load Risk Across the Field

**[TOPIC]:** Violence exposure (contextual, personal, weapon-specific, cumulative) and its association with depressive symptoms and cardiovascular/allostatic-load outcomes from adolescence through midlife.

**[MY RESEARCH QUESTION]:** Do county-level violent crime and personally reported exposure show comparable within-person associations with depressive symptoms; do exposure course and weapon involvement clarify the personal-exposure signal and persist within siblings/twins; and is prior exposure history associated with cardiovascular and multisystem biomarker change, including within siblings?
