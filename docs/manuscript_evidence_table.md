# Research Evidence Table

**Source manuscript:** Abbas, J. *Violence Exposure Is Not a Unitary Construct: Contextual Crime, Personal Exposure, Depression, and Cardiovascular Risk from Adolescence to Midlife* (revised manuscript, prepared for submission to *Social Science & Medicine*).

**My research question (as stated in the manuscript):** Do distinct violence-exposure measures — ambient county-level crime, personally reported victimization/witnessing, exposure course (first-observed/recurrent/post-exposure), and weapon-specific exposure — produce distinct psychological (depressive-symptom) and physiological (cardiovascular, allostatic-load) signatures from adolescence through midlife, and do the observed associations persist across individual, sibling, and monozygotic-twin fixed-effects comparisons?

**Extraction rule.** Every cell below reflects only what the manuscript's text states about the cited work. Nothing has been supplemented from the original source papers, which were not separately retrieved. Where the manuscript does not report a detail, the cell reads **Not reported**.
