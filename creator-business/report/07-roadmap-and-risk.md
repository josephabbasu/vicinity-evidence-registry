## Risk register

| Risk | L×I | Mitigation | Early signal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Algorithm/policy shift hits finance or AI-assisted content | M×H | Owned newsletter list from day 1; human-voiced, disclosed pipeline; diversify to course/licensing | Impression share drop >30% mo/mo |
| YMYL demonetization | L×H | Education framing, no product recommendations, advertiser-friendly language checks in SEO module | Yellow icons on >10% uploads |
| Trust incident (a wrong claim goes viral) | L×VH | Fact-check gate, adversarial review pass, public corrections ritual (converts errors into trust) | Any correction — run the ritual |
| Slow growth / founder burnout | M×M | Batch buffer, 12–24mo expectation, pipeline reduces per-video hours ~50% | Buffer <1 week for 3 weeks |
| Copycats | H×L | Moat = trust record + library depth + community localization data; copying the format ≠ copying 3 years of receipts | — |
| Regulatory (financial promotion rules, e.g., UK FCA) | L×M | No product promotion; jurisdiction disclaimers; legal review before course & sponsor categories | Sponsor category inquiries |
| Key-person dependence on host | M×M | Build format > face; second presenter by year 2; documented pipeline (this repo) | — |
| AI cost/quality drift | L×M | CreatorOS prompt library versioned; monthly eval of outputs vs rubric | Fact-check failure rate rising |

## Growth experiment backlog (beyond 90 days)

Community-sourced localization series · collab matrix with credentialed experts (economists, consumer lawyers) · Shorts remix of top retention segments · seasonal packages (January Reset, tax seasons by country, graduation) · quiz-format community posts as subscriber activators · podcast feed of VO tracks (zero marginal cost) · auto-dub expansion by watch-time geography.

## Final recommendation

Proceed. The niche decision is robust across scoring weights; the differentiation is structural; the production system keeps the trust promise auditable; and the revenue portfolio doesn't depend on any single stream or geography. The binding constraint is consistent execution for 12–24 months — which the CreatorOS pipeline (Phase 6, built in this repository) is designed to make sustainable.
