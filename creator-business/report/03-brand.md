# Phase 3 — Brand System

## Channel name options

| Name | Rationale | Risk check |
|---|---|---|
| **Plain Money** ⭐ recommended | Says the promise (plain language, no agenda) in two words; global-English friendly; extensible (Plain Money Kids, Plain Money en Español) | Common words — trademark search needed per class; handle `@plainmoney` availability to verify |
| Money, Explained | Instantly clear; strong search association | Collides with Vox's Netflix series title |
| The Money Compass | Navigation metaphor, brandable mark | Slightly generic in fintech |
| First Principles Money | Precise positioning | Long; nerdy for mass audience |
| Common Cents | Warm, memorable | Pun doesn't translate globally |
| MoneyGround | "Common ground" + fundamentals | Abstract |
| The Ledger Project | Editorial gravitas | "Ledger" owned mentally by crypto brand |
| ClearWorth | Brandable, ownable | Meaning less obvious |

**Decision: Plain Money** (fallback: The Money Compass). Verify handle/trademark before launch — action item in the 30-day plan.

## Mission

> Give every person on Earth a free, trustworthy money education — in language anyone can understand.

## Vision

> A world where no one is poorer for what nobody taught them.

## Value proposition

**For viewers:** understand any money concept in one video — principle first, your country second, sources always.
**For sponsors/partners:** the most trusted brand-safe context in the highest-value vertical on YouTube.
**For educators/NGOs:** a citable, licensable curriculum that already works on the platform students live on.

## Unique positioning

The **trust brand** in a low-trust niche; the **global** channel in a US-centric niche; the **principles** channel in a tips-and-hacks niche. One sentence: *"The money channel with receipts."*

## Brand voice

| Attribute | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|
| Plain | Short sentences. Concrete examples. Define every term at first use. | Jargon, acronyms without expansion |
| Calm | "Here's how this works." | Urgency, fear, hype, ALL CAPS promises |
| Honest | "Here's what the evidence says — and where it's uncertain." | Overclaiming, cherry-picking |
| Respectful | Assume intelligence, acknowledge constraints ("if money is tight…") | Latte-shaming, bootstraps moralizing |
| Global | "In some countries… what's it called in yours?" | US-default assumptions |

Reading level: global English, ~B1–B2 (roughly grade 7–8). Numbers always visualized. Humor: dry, sparing, never at the viewer's expense.

## Tagline

**"Money, explained. For everyone, everywhere."**
Short forms: *"Explained, with receipts."* · Series stinger: *"Now you know how it works."*

## Visual identity

**Concept: "The Clear Ledger"** — clean paper-and-ink editorial style (think financial newspaper redesigned by a modern app), signaling credibility + clarity. Not neon-crypto, not corporate-bank blue-suit.

### Color palette

| Role | Color | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary | Ledger Ink | `#0F172A` | Text, dark surfaces |
| Background | Paper | `#F8F7F2` | Light backgrounds, thumbnails |
| Accent | Verified Green | `#0E9F6E` | Positive numbers, "verified" checkmark, CTA |
| Accent 2 | Signal Amber | `#F59E0B` | Warnings, scam-series accent, highlight underlines |
| Support | Slate | `#64748B` | Secondary text, chart gridlines |
| Alert | Claret | `#B91C1C` | Losses, myth-busting "FALSE" stamps |

Accessibility: all text pairs meet WCAG AA at their intended sizes; green/amber/claret never used as the only differentiator (icons + labels accompany).

### Typography

- **Headlines / thumbnails:** Archivo Black or Inter Tight ExtraBold — high-x-height, legible at 120px-wide thumbnail scale.
- **Body / captions / lower-thirds:** Inter (400/600).
- **Numbers & ledger motifs:** IBM Plex Mono — monospace evokes receipts/ledgers; used for all on-screen figures.
- All three are open-source (Google Fonts) → zero licensing friction, embeddable in app and thumbnails.

### Logo concepts

1. **The Receipt Check** ⭐ — a minimal receipt outline whose jagged bottom edge resolves into a checkmark; monochrome ink on paper. Scales to favicon; the check doubles as the "verified" motif in videos.
2. **PM Ledger Mark** — "PM" set in Plex Mono inside a rounded ledger cell, with a green underline like a verified total.
3. **The Open Book Coin** — coin circle whose inner detail is an open book; classic education × money synthesis (more conventional fallback).

### Thumbnail style guide

- Paper background (`#F8F7F2`) or Ink background (`#0F172A`) — alternate to create shelf rhythm; nothing else in the niche looks like this (everyone else: red arrows on money photos).
- **One idea per thumbnail:** ≤4 words, Archivo Black, ink or paper color.
- One large visual object (isolated 3D/flat illustration: a coin, a padlock, a shrinking banknote) — never stock photos of cash fans.
- Signature elements: green ✓ or claret ✗ stamp; amber marker underline on the key word; monospace numerals when a number is the hook.
- Face usage: host face in ~30% of thumbnails (verdict/reaction formats) for authenticity signals; consistent left-third placement.
- Legibility test enforced in CreatorOS checklist: readable at 160×90 px.

### Intro animation concept (≤3 seconds)

Ink line draws a receipt; numbers roll up in Plex Mono; the total line snaps into the green checkmark; wordmark "PLAIN MONEY" stamps on paper with a subtle press sound. Audio sting: two-note marimba + till "cha-ching" pitched down (calm, not gimmicky). Shorts use a 0.7-second version (stamp + sting only).

## Brand architecture (future-proofing)

- **Plain Money** (flagship channel) → **Plain Money Shorts-native clips** (same channel) → **Plain Money Newsletter** → **Money Foundations** (course) → **Plain Money Kids / language editions** (year 3+ expansions, same visual system, new accent colors per edition).
