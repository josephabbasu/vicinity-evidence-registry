# Phase 7 — Business Model & Revenue Streams

## Portfolio strategy

Ad revenue is the *floor*, not the business. The brand asset (trust) is monetized through streams that don't erode it, sequenced so each stream funds the next. Geography-insensitive streams (course, tools, licensing) deliberately offset the global audience's lower blended RPM.

## Revenue model scenarios (year 3, annual)

Assumptions: 🟡 medium confidence; sensitivity ±40%. PPP pricing reduces course ARPU but expands volume.

|---|---|---|---|

| Sponsorships (2/mo → 6/mo, $4k → $12k) | $95k | $290k | $860k |

| Memberships + community | $20k | $60k | $140k |

| Licensing + B2B | $20k | $80k | $250k |
| **Total ARR** | **$330k** | **$940k** | **$2.46M** |

Cost structure at base case: host/editor-in-chief, video editor, part-time researcher/community manager, tools & AI inference (~$400–800/mo), music/stock licenses → **60–75% operating margin** typical for lean creator businesses at this scale. 🟡

## Pricing philosophy

- **Free tier is the mission** (videos, newsletter, 1-page plan) — reach and trust are the assets.
- **Paid tiers sell structure and convenience,** never secrets ("everything in the course is on the channel; the course is the fastest path through it").
- **PPP pricing** for the course/app (e.g., 100% / 60% / 35% price bands by region) — aligns with the global mission and expands TAM instead of leaking it.

## Flywheel

```
 Videos (trust) ─▶ Subscribers ─▶ Newsletter (owned) ─▶ Course & tools ($)
      ▲                                                       │
      │                                                       ▼
 Reinvest in quality ◀── Sponsorships ($) ◀── Reach ◀── Licensing spreads brand
```

Rule: every dollar stream must *increase* the reason viewers trust the free content, or it is rejected — that discipline **is** the long-term brand.
